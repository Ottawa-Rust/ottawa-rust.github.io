# History of Ottawa Rust

The Ottawa Rust group started on MeetUp in 2016 and had 4 very well
attended meetings.

![Ottawa Rust first meetup](/img/meetup-1.png)

It then went into hibernation until 2024 when it was resurrected. Just
before the 3rd meeting after hibernation, the MeetUp group got deleted
due to a mixup of the handoff between organizers.

[Check out the first MeetUp group on archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20241012191614/https://www.meetup.com/Ottawa-Rust-Language-Meetup/)

But like a good crab, we've molted again. This time we're rebuilding
around [our website](https://ottawa-rust.ca), our [zulip
chat](https://ottawa-rust.zulipchat.com/) and a new [meetup
group](https://www.meetup.com/meetup-group-bdcioynp/). Say hello in
chat and show up to our next meeting!
